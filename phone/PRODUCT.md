# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The owner of JustDay, primarily using an Android phone to control and stay connected to his own Linux computers. He uses the phone both upright for JustDay and rotated sideways as a Stream Deck. The interface must be understandable at a glance and comfortable to operate by touch.

## Product Purpose

JustDay on the phone is the mobile companion to the desktop assistant: speak or type to JustDay, control household computers, and continue a `justday terminal` task remotely with visible progress. In landscape it becomes a separate Stream Deck surface. Success means the owner can reach the right task quickly, understand whether it is running, and safely continue it while away from the computer.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, capable, direct. It should feel light and carefully finished, with the clarity and restraint the owner associates with Apple interfaces, while remaining recognizably JustDay and honest about connection and task state.

## Anti-references

- The existing phone layout is not a visual source of truth for the requested redesign.
- No crowded control panels, decorative cards without a job, hidden task state, or fake progress.
- Avoid generic “AI app” tropes and any CouCou/Mochi names, characters, artwork, sounds, or screen designs.
- Do not hide security boundaries: remote access is through the owner's private Tailscale network; never expose a new JustDay listener to the LAN or internet.

## Design Principles

1. Two clear destinations: JustDay and Stream Deck.
2. Show the task's current state and recent progress before offering input to continue it.
3. Keep routine control immediate; make consequential actions explicit.
4. Keep the interface light, readable, touch-friendly, and useful at a glance.
5. Make offline, waiting, failed, and completed states truthful and distinct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard operation where a physical keyboard is available, visible focus, readable contrast checked numerically, reduced-motion support, and controls with accessible names. Touch targets and text must remain usable on narrow phone screens and when the display is magnified.
