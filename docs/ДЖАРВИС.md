## JARVIS — FINAL REQUIREMENTS

Build JARVIS as my main personal AI assistant. Treat the assistant as a unified system, not just a chatbot.

CORE ACCESS & AUTONOMY
- JARVIS has broad access to my computer, files, apps, calendar, location and available tools.
- It should act autonomously and make routine decisions without constantly asking me.
- Ask for confirmation only before genuinely dangerous/irreversible actions:
  - permanent file deletion;
  - modifying critical JARVIS/core configuration;
  - changing important system files/configuration that could break the system;
  - other actions with serious irreversible consequences.
- If I explicitly instructed JARVIS to perform an action, do it without unnecessary confirmation.

CALENDAR
- Use Google Calendar.
- The calendar is effectively "ours", not something JARVIS merely reads.
- JARVIS may create, edit, move, cancel and manage events autonomously.
- Meeting reminders must be dynamic.
- For online meetings, ~10–20 minutes beforehand is usually enough.
- For physical meetings, use my phone location + route/travel time to calculate when I need to be warned.
- Do NOT use a fixed "1 hour before" rule.
- If a meeting is at night, warn me sufficiently BEFORE I go to sleep so I know about it in advance.
- Meetings should never simply be ignored because it is nighttime.

URGENCY & COMMUNICATION
- Normal communication starts with a message/notification.
- If I do not respond and the situation is genuinely urgent, JARVIS may call me.
- Do not call me for trivial matters.
- If something is not urgent, JARVIS decides the appropriate communication method itself.
- If a meeting needs action and I do not respond, JARVIS may decide whether to reschedule/cancel it when appropriate.
- For calls, choose the cheapest reliable option (cellular, Telegram, VoIP, etc.), not merely the easiest one to implement.

CONTEXT & LISTENING
- JARVIS may continuously understand my environment and conversations when technically possible.
- It may listen to meetings/conferences and remember relevant context, promises, commitments and action items.
- It should understand what I am doing, where I am and what I am saying.
- Passwords/secrets must remain protected and must never be exposed through normal memory/context output. Use the existing protected credential mechanism instead.

LOCATION
- JARVIS may use my phone's location.
- Use it intelligently for things such as estimating travel time and whether I will make a meeting.
- Do not use simplistic fixed-time reminders when real location/travel information is available.

INTERFACES
- The dedicated JARVIS application is the PRIMARY interface.
- It contains the main chat/messages, computer control and JARVIS controls.
- Telegram is a FULL BACKUP interface, not merely a notification channel.
- Telegram should duplicate the important functionality so JARVIS remains accessible if the main application fails, I change phones, or someone needs temporary/emergency access.
- Telegram may also be useful for emergency communication/calls.
- Choose the actual calling technology based on ongoing cost/reliability; cheapest reliable option wins.

AI / API
- I already have an OpenRouter API key and can provide it again if necessary.
- Do not assume I need to buy another key.
- RAM stays at 16 GB for now. DO NOT design the system around upgrading to 64 GB or require a RAM upgrade.
- Prefer efficient/local models where practical, but work within the existing 16 GB machine.

UI
- The top interface should use an island/dynamic-island style as the baseline.
- The implementation can start wherever technically convenient, but the intended visual direction is the island.
- Do not overcomplicate the top bar with unnecessary information.
- Keep the interface clean and functional.

GENERAL RULE
JARVIS should feel like one coherent personal operating system/assistant:
- autonomous by default;
- asks only when something is genuinely dangerous or consequential;
- remembers useful context;
- uses location/context intelligently;
- communicates through the best available channel;
- keeps the dedicated app as the primary interface;
- keeps Telegram as a robust fallback.

Do not repeatedly ask me questions that are already answered above. Infer obvious decisions from these requirements and continue implementation.

## Что из этого следует для нас, по-русски

**Что он этим закрыл — больше не спрашивать:**

- Календарь — **Google, и он общий**: заводить, править, переносить и отменять встречи можно
  самому. Жёсткого «за час» нет: онлайн — за 10–20 минут, живая встреча — по дороге и его
  положению, ночная встреча — предупредить **до того, как он ляжет**.
- Связь: сначала сообщение, звонок — только если не ответил и дело правда срочное. Чем звонить —
  выбираем по цене и надёжности, а не по простоте написания кода.
- Слушать окружение и встречи — **можно**, помнить обещания и договорённости — нужно. Пароли при
  этом не должны попадать ни в память, ни в контекст: для них есть связка ключей.
- Положение телефона — **можно** и нужно, именно для «успеешь ли».
- Память: 16 ГБ и ничего не покупать. Ключ OpenRouter у него уже есть.
- Островок остаётся основным видом верхней полосы, и грузить его лишним не надо.
- Подтверждения — только на правда опасное: безвозвратное удаление, правка важного в системе и в
  самом Джарвисе, необратимое. Сказал сделать — делать без переспрашивания.

**Что здесь новое и чего у нас не было совсем:**

1. **Своё приложение Джарвиса — главный способ им пользоваться.** В нём разговор, управление
   компьютером и управление самим Джарвисом. У нас сейчас есть только пульт на телефоне
   (`phone/`: файлы и снимки экрана) и островок на компьютере.
2. **Telegram — полноценная запасная оболочка**, а не канал уведомлений: то же самое, что в
   приложении, на случай если приложение упало, телефон сменился или нужен срочный доступ.
3. **Звонки по цене**: не «что проще», а что дешевле и надёжнее — сотовая связь, Telegram, VoIP.
